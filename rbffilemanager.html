Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rabbitfoot mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed and operated by Rabbitfoot ("we," "us," or "our"), based in India. This document explains how information is handled when you use our App.

Core Privacy Promise: Rabbitfoot is designed to process your files, photos, videos, and documents entirely on your local device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal files or media content to external cloud servers.

1. On-Device File Processing

All core functions of the App—including duplicate detection, similar photo scanning, large file analysis, photo/video compression, AI photo restoration, sensitive photo identification, and archive/ZIP generation—are executed 100% locally on your device using on-device machine learning and file processing routines.

  • Your files, photos, videos, and credentials never leave your device through our software.
  • We do not operate backend servers that store your personal data or uploaded media.

2. Device Permissions Requested

To provide file management, storage cleaning, and compression capabilities, the App may request access to device storage permissions, such as:

  • Storage / Media Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to scan, locate duplicates, analyze storage space, compress media, manage archives, and perform deletion tasks upon your explicit instructions.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App is supported through advertisements served by Google AdMob. While Rabbitfoot does not directly collect personal data, Google AdMob may collect and process certain data to display personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Data collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • Advertising IDs (e.g., Android Advertising ID).
  • IP address and coarse location data.
  • App interaction data, crash logs, and diagnostic performance metrics.

For detailed information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit Google's privacy disclosures:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use basic diagnostic tools provided by Google Play or third-party SDKs to analyze app stability and performance. This data is strictly anonymous and aggregated, containing no personally identifiable information (PII) or user file contents.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Security

Since your files remain stored exclusively on your device, the security of your files relies on the security measures provided by your Android operating system and your personal device protection settings (e.g., screen locks, passcodes).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page regularly for any modifications.
