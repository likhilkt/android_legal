Quick File Share

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026. Published by RabbitFoot, India.

In short
  • Your files never pass through our servers. We don’t run any. Files go directly between your phone and the device you connect, over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  • We don’t collect your name, contacts, files or location.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to serve and measure ads. You can control personalised ads.

1. Who we are

Quick File Share (the “App”) is published by RabbitFoot (“we”, “us”), based in India. If you have questions about this policy, email likhil004play@gmail.com.

2. What the App does

The App runs a small file server on your phone. Another device on the same Wi-Fi network, or connected to your phone’s hotspot, can open the address shown in the App in any web browser, enter a 6-digit passcode, and then view or download the files you chose to share. If you turn on “Allow uploads”, that device can also send files to your phone.

4. Permissions the App uses

  • All files access (only if you choose “Share all”): to list and serve files from your phone’s storage to devices you allow. If you use “Selected items”, the App only reads the files and folders you pick through Android’s file picker.
  • Storage write (Android 9 and older, only if you allow uploads): to save received files in Downloads.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state, internet: to find your phone’s local address, run the file server, and load ads.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep sharing running in the background and show a notification with a Stop button.
  • Wake lock: to keep transfers from stopping when the screen turns off.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To serve, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Interactions with ads, and app performance and crash diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by RabbitFoot, and is handled under Google’s privacy policy. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where required by US state laws, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time from “Privacy settings” in the App.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings, under Google, then Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Ad-free hour: you can watch an optional rewarded video to turn off ads for one hour. This is stored only on your phone.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t upload, copy, scan or keep your files.
  • We don’t sell your personal data.
  • We don’t require an account or sign-in.

7. Security

Every sharing session uses a new random 6-digit passcode, and a device is locked out for a minute after 5 wrong attempts. Sharing stops as soon as you tap Stop. Transfers travel over your local network using plain HTTP and are not encrypted, so use the App on your own Wi-Fi or hotspot rather than on public networks, and share the passcode only with people you trust.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Users under 18 should use the App with the permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Your rights

Because RabbitFoot does not collect or store your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data on your phone can be removed by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it. For data collected by Google for ads, use the controls described in section 5. If you have a concern, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact us at likhil004play@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
