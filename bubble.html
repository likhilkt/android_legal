Bubble Drift – Privacy Policy

November 10, 2026 · @Likhil

Who we are

Bubble Drift (package rbf.bubble.drift.game) is a free puzzle game for Android made by RabbitFoot ("we", "us"). We do not collect, store or sell your personal data ourselves. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve them, as described below.

Effective date: 10 November 2026.

Information collected

We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, sound settings, ad-free timer) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) built into the game automatically collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention (Google's disclosure):

Data What it is
IP address Used by Google to estimate your approximate (city-level) location
Device identifiers Android advertising ID and app set ID
App interactions App launches, taps on ads, video ad views
Diagnostics App and SDK performance, such as launch time and hang rate

This data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We never receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports in our AdMob account.

Ads and your consent

The game shows full-screen ads after a game over and optional rewarded videos (to unlock a level or get 30 ad-free minutes). Google may use the data above to show personalized ads, unless you decline.

  • Consent form: In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are requested.
  • Change your choice: Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options (shown where required).
  • Opt out on your device: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization on older versions).

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy .

Permissions, children, retention and security

Permissions. The game uses Internet and network state (to load ads), vibration (game feedback) and the advertising ID (added by the AdMob SDK). It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children. Bubble Drift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it with Google.

Retention and deletion. Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google is kept under Google's retention policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings.

Security. We store nothing on servers. Ad data is sent by the Google SDK over encrypted connections.

International transfers. Google may process ad data in countries other than yours, under its own safeguards.

Your rights and changes

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the US), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use for personalized ads.

Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by Google: use Privacy options in the game, your Android ad settings, or Google's privacy controls . For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.
