# Privacy Policy — PDF Tools Pro **Effective date:** 14 October 2026 **Developer:** RabbitFoot **App:** PDF Tools Pro (package `pdf.tools.ai.rabbitfoot`) **Contact:** [your-email@example.com](mailto:your-email@example.com) This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Tools Pro ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built the app so that **your documents stay on your device**. --- ## 1. Summary - **Your files are processed on your device.** PDFs, images, scans, signatures and form data are never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate any servers that receive your files. - **We do not require an account** and we do not collect your name, email or phone number. - **The app shows ads** through Google AdMob. Ads use limited device information, as described in section 5. - **Some features use Google Play services** (document scanner and text recognition). These run on your device, but Google may download models and collect limited technical data, as described in section 4. - **Sensitive data you choose to store** (Vault files and your Auto-fill profile) is encrypted on your device. --- ## 2. Google Play services (document scanner and text recognition) The document scanner and text recognition are provided by **Google ML Kit** through Google Play services: - Images are processed **on your device**. They are not sent to Google for processing. - Google Play services may download the required models over the internet. - Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and performance metrics, to operate and improve these services. See [Google's ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). --- ## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob) The app is free and supported by ads from **Google AdMob**. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use: - Your device's advertising ID - Device and app information (for example model, operating system version, language) - IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location - Interactions with ads (for example views and clicks) **Consent:** where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time from **Menu → Privacy options** on the home screen, when that option is shown. **Your controls:** - You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings: **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (the exact path varies by device). Deleting it opts you out of personalised ads. - Rewarded ads are always optional. Learn more about how Google uses information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). **Your documents are never used for advertising** and are never shared with ad providers. --- ## 6. Sharing of information We do not sell, rent or share your information. Your files leave the app **only when you choose to**, for example when you tap **Share**, **Save**, **Save as**, **Open with another app** or **Print**. In those cases, the file goes to the app or location you select, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Information may be processed by Google as described in sections 4 and 5. --- ## 7. Data retention and deletion - **Temporary files** (working copies, exports waiting to be shared) are stored in the app's cache and deleted automatically, usually within a day. Decrypted Vault copies are deleted when the app restarts. - **Recent files:** remove an item by long-pressing it, or tap **Clear** on the home screen. - **Vault:** delete files from inside the Vault. - **Auto-fill profile:** tap **Clear all details** in the profile screen. - **Edit PDF document:** use **Close document** in Edit PDF. - **Everything:** uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in **Settings → Apps → PDF Tools Pro → Storage → Clear storage**, permanently deletes all data held by the app. Files you saved to your Downloads or Pictures folders remain there until you delete them. --- ## 8. Security - All app data is kept in Android's private, sandboxed app storage, which other apps cannot access. - Vault files, the Vault file list, and the Auto-fill profile are additionally encrypted with AES-256 using a key stored in the Android Keystore. - The Vault locks automatically when the app goes to the background. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we design the app to keep your data on your device and protected. --- ## 9. Children's privacy The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the law in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are not intended to target children. --- ## 10. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. For data processed by Google for ads, you can use Google's controls described in section 5. For any other question, contact us using the details below. --- ## 11.
