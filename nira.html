Privacy Policy - Nira

Package: nira.game.puzzle.rbf
Last updated: November 8, 2026

What stays on your device

Match history, credits, ad-free timer and the sound setting are stored only on your device. We do not run any server and do not collect your name, email or account data.

Multiplayer

Games with a friend run directly between two devices over your local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth. No game data is sent to us.

Ads

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads and, where you consent, to personalise them.

In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you are asked for consent on first launch, and you can change your choice from "Ad privacy settings" on the home screen when shown.

More information: Google Partner Sites Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Information .

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
