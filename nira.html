# Privacy Policy - Nira (nira.game.puzzle.rbf) Last updated: Nov, 8, 2026 Contact: likhil004play@gmail.com **What stays on your device.** Match history, credits, ad-free timer and the sound setting are stored only on your device. We do not run any server and do not collect your name, email or account data. **Multiplayer.** Games with a friend run directly between two devices over your local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth. No game data is sent to us. **Ads.** The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads and, where you consent, to personalise them. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you are asked for consent on first launch, and you can change your choice from "Ad privacy settings" on the home screen when shown. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 **Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13. **Your choices.** You can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device. **Changes.** We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
