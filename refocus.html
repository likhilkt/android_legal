Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Refocus (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. “We” means RabbitFoot, the developer of the App.

The short version
  • Your photos stay on your phone. Enhancement, face detection and colorization run on your device. We never receive, upload or store your photos.
  • No account, no sign-up, and no analytics or tracking of our own.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain device and usage data as described in section 3.
  • A photo is written to your gallery only when you tap Save or Share.

1.Information we do not collect

We do not run servers for the App and we do not collect, receive or sell personal information. In particular, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the photos you process. There are no user accounts.

2.How the App handles your photos and faces

  • Photo access. You choose a photo with the Android system photo picker, or share one to the App from another app. The App can only read the photo you select; it does not request storage, camera or location permissions.
  • On-device processing. The photo is decoded and processed in your phone’s memory using AI models that run locally (LiteRT / TensorFlow Lite format). The photo is never sent over the network.
  • Face detection. To find faces, the App uses Google ML Kit’s on-device face detector. It only locates faces and their landmark points (eyes, mouth) in the picture so each face can be restored. The App does not identify or recognise people, does not create or store face templates or biometric identifiers, and does not keep the detection results after processing.
  • Results. The enhanced photo exists only in memory until you tap Save (it is then written to Pictures/Refocus on your device) or Share (it is saved there and handed to the app you pick). Anything you share onward is governed by the policy of that app.
  • Local settings. Your preferences (for example maximum output size and options) and the AI model files are stored in the App’s private storage on your device. Uninstalling the App removes them. Saved photos in your gallery remain until you delete them.

3.Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show a banner ad on every screen and an interstitial (full-screen) ad after you save a photo. The App needs the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions for this.

When ads are requested and shown, Google and its ad partners may collect and process, directly from your device:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID (or an app-set ID when the advertising ID is unavailable or reset);
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type);
  • ad interaction data (ad requests, impressions, clicks) and app-level performance and crash diagnostics.

This is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep the service working. We do not receive this data and cannot see it in a form that identifies you; Google acts as an independent controller for it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • On Android, open Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization. Ads are then not personalised, but you may still see ads.
  • You can manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires your consent for personalised advertising or for storing/accessing information on your device, ads are shown according to the choice you make in Google’s consent message, and you can change that choice later where the App provides the option.

5.Data retention and security

We hold no user data. Everything the App stores (settings, model files) stays on your device. Downloads use HTTPS. Because processing is local, the security of your photos depends mainly on the security of your device.

6.Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and use your device’s ad settings to limit ad personalization.

7.Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights regarding data that Google handles for advertising; you can exercise them through Google’s tools linked in section 3. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use or disclose it for purposes other than described here.
