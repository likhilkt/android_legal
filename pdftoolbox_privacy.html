Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026. Applies to the Android app PDF Toolbox (package pdf.tools.ai.rabbitfoot).

Your documents are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us.

  • No account is needed. We don't collect your name, email or phone number.
  • The Vault and your auto-fill details are encrypted on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses limited device information (see section 5).
  • The scanner and text recognition run on your device using Google Play services (see section 4).

The app does not request access to your storage, contacts, location, microphone or camera:

  • You choose files and photos with Android's file and photo pickers, which only give the app the items you select.
  • Scanning uses Google's document scanner or your camera app.
  • Saved files go to your Downloads or Pictures folder through Android's standard storage system.

3How your files are processed

Everything the app does to your documents happens on your device, including text recognition (OCR), text editing, redaction, conversion and compression.

When you make an ID card copy, the Aadhaar number is found and masked on your device. The hidden digits are removed from the image in the resulting PDF, not just covered.

4Google Play services

The document scanner and text recognition are provided by Google ML Kit through Google Play services.

  • Images are processed on your device and are not sent to Google for processing.
  • Google Play services may download the required models over the internet.
  • Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app details and performance metrics, to run and improve these services.

Read more in Google's ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

5Advertising

PDF Toolbox is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information, such as model, Android version and language
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice any time from the home screen menu under Privacy options, when that option is shown.

Your controls

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads. Deleting it turns off personalised ads.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Your documents are never used for advertising and are never shared with ad providers. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: Google partner sites policy.

6Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your information.

Your files leave the app only when you choose to share, save, open them in another app or print them. They then go to the app or place you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Google processes information as described in section 4 and section 5.

7Keeping and deleting data

  • Temporary files are deleted automatically, usually within a day. Decrypted Vault copies are deleted when the app restarts.
  • Recent files: long-press an item to remove it, or tap Clear on the home screen.
  • Vault: delete files from inside the Vault.
  • Auto-fill profile: tap Clear all details on the profile screen.
  • Edit PDF: tap Close document.
  • Everything: uninstall the app, or go to Settings > Apps > PDF Toolbox > Storage > Clear storage.

Files you saved to your Downloads or Pictures folder stay there until you delete them.

8Security

  • All app data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps can't access.
  • Vault files, the list of Vault files, and the auto-fill profile are also encrypted with AES-256 using a key stored in the Android Keystore.
  • The Vault locks automatically when the app goes to the background.

No storage method is completely secure, but the app is designed to keep your data on your device and protected.

9Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13, or the minimum age set by law in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads in the app aren't intended to target children.

10Your rights

Your documents and details are stored only on your device, so you can view, export or delete them at any time using the options in section 7.

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU or UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For ad data processed by Google, use the controls in section 5. For anything else, contact us.
