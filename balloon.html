Privacy Policy

Balloon Pottikkal (Android app, package balloon.kerala.game.rbf)
Developer: RabbitFoot, India
Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Balloon Pottikkal game ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built the app to collect as little as possible. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your phone: unlocked levels, stars, chosen character, sound setting, tutorial status and the time an ad-free period ends. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Interactions with ads, and app performance and crash diagnostics

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies. We do not receive information from AdMob that identifies you personally. Please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Rewarded video ads are always optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as unlocking a level or 30 ad-free minutes.

3. Your consent choices

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the app's main menu, where it is available in your region.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

4. Bluetooth multiplayer and permissions

The optional multiplayer mode connects phones that are near each other using Bluetooth. To find and connect to nearby phones, Android asks for:

  • Nearby devices (Android 12 and newer)
  • Location (Android 8 to 11 only). Older versions of Android require this permission for any app that scans for Bluetooth devices. The app does not read, collect or store your location.

During a multiplayer game, the phones exchange only game information, such as player moves, positions, chosen characters and scores. This information travels directly between the connected phones. It is not sent to us or to any server, and it is not stored after the game ends. The app also shows the Bluetooth names of nearby phones so you can choose whom to join; these names are not saved or shared.

The app uses the Internet permission only to load ads and consent forms.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the information collected by Google AdMob as described above, the app does not share information with third parties.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data security and retention

Game progress is kept only on your device until you delete it. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's retention policies. Multiplayer game data is not retained after a match.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to raise a complaint. This includes rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, and US state privacy laws. Because we do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, most requests about ad data are best handled through your Android ad settings and Google's tools linked above. You are always welcome to contact us, and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
